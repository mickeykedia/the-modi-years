# Sourcing policy

- Prefer primary sources for what the government did: statutes, rules, notifications, budgets, judgments, parliamentary material, audit reports, and official datasets.
- Use independent research and reporting for implementation, outcomes, distributional effects, and disputed claims.
- Record the exact claim supported by each source.
- Preserve publication date, retrieval date, publisher, source type, and archived URL when available.
- Do not treat a government announcement as evidence that its intended outcome occurred.
- Do not treat a single secondary report as settled evidence for a contested causal claim.
- Mark broken links, retractions, methodological concerns, and conflicts between sources.
- A record with no claim-level sources cannot be marked `reviewed`.
