# Scoring policy

The score expresses the editor's overall value judgement, not a measurement or public-opinion estimate.

| Score | Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `+2` | Beneficial | Benefits strongly outweigh harms and trade-offs. |
| `+1` | More good | Benefits outweigh harms, with material qualifications. |
| `0` | Unjudged or balanced | Not yet assessed, or no responsible overall judgement is available. |
| `-1` | More bad | Harms outweigh benefits, with a credible positive case. |
| `-2` | Harmful | Harms strongly outweigh benefits or violate a core constraint. |

Every non-zero score must name the relevant values, affected groups, confidence, rationale, and strongest countercase. Significance controls display size; it must never alter the score.

Do not calculate a single government-wide score until the project has a documented selection rule and an independently defensible weighting method.
